Next moves, in order

Each one unblocks the next. The Studio decision means little until the mini is back up and has room to work.

  1. Bring the Mac mini back online Today

    Your August 21 note: after it loses power, plugging it back in isn't enough. It needs the power button on the back.

    Press the rear power button, then confirm the Daily Brief print and Open Brain jobs run. Stop it happening again: System Settings → Energy → turn on Start up automatically after a power failure. Optional: a small UPS ($60–100) keeps it running through blips.

  2. Give it room to work This week

    In June it generated 240 GB in five days. With 37 GB free, Xcode builds and agent runs start failing in odd ways. Target 100 GB free.

    Clear Xcode build caches: delete ~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData. Remove old simulators: xcrun simctl delete unavailable. Move Codex video and finished project folders to an external 2 TB SSD or OneDrive. Keep active repos local.
